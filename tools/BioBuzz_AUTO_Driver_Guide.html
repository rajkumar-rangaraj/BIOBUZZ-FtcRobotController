🐝 AUTO: in the match

what the robot does · hands off · if something goes wrong · checklist

⏱️ What the robot does by itself (30 s)

10–1 s🚀LEAVELeaves the wall, spins up the flywheel.LEAVE +3 (counted at the end)
21–7 s🎯SHOOTCamera aims at our up CELL, fires the 4 pre-loads.HIVE TIP +20 if it tips
37–14 s🌼GARDENSweeps slowly along the wall into the GARDEN corner, intake on.POLLEN for TeleOp
414–18 s↔️CORNERIntake on up to 4 s, going 2 in back and forth, so the last POLLEN rolls in.
5~18 s🅿️PARKIn our LOADING ZONE. Slot B: parked by ~24 s. Slot A: by ~28 s.PARK +5
STRETCH (○)🔁OFF by defaultBack to the spot, fires the GARDEN POLLEN only if the camera sees our CELL still up.
Times are from S1 with no start delay. A start delay makes it all later. From S2 it first drives about 5 s to the shot spot.
🅿️ PARKING ALWAYS WINS: at the Park-by time (24 s, or earlier if the drive is long) it stops what it is doing and parks.
✋ During AUTO: hands off
  • Don't touch the robot or the gamepads. The gamepads do nothing in AUTO.
  • Don't press STOP. All motors stop at once and the robot won't park. Emergencies only.
  • It waits or skips a step? Let it. Those are its own rules (middle column).
  • Watch the Step line to see what it is doing.
➡️ After AUTO: TeleOp
  1. The 30 s AUTO timer ends AUTO. The robot holds its park until then.
  2. BioBuzz TeleOp is already selected for you: press INIT.
  3. Alliance line: AUTO already set our colour. Check it anyway.
  4. Field-centric on? The screen says Start heading: from AUTO. Don't press OPTIONS.
  5. Don't touch the robot. ▶ START when TeleOp starts.
